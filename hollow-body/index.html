Hollow Body

Review Screenshots

Review

Hollow Body is an indie Silent Hill style survival horror game. It manages to pull it off reasonably well though it is a bit rough around the edges.

The gameplay is pretty serviceable though I did require a walkthrough for a few of it's puzzles. The combat works but is nothing special.

The story was pretty engaging but was also confusing to me and by the end I was struggling to follow along. It seems to be interesting if you can follow it, and it also seems like there are multiple endings like Silent Hill.

I liked the art style, kind of ps2 era graphics with abandond decaying city mixed with sort of body horror.

I would say it's worth it if you are big into Silent Hill style games.
