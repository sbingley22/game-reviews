Quite a unique game where you play as a detective recalled from banishment to a paradise Island where the main characters are practically gods scheming for power, or at least some are.

I really quite enjoyed this, talking to the characters, learning their backstories, trying to workout out who would have motive and ability to pull off a murder of a god. The characters are all unique and fun.

The gameplay consists of running around the Island, solving puzzles, collecting items, and interacting with characters. You gain abilities and entry to different places as you progress. I found it quite enjoyable moving around the island, learning about its inhabitants and social structure just from observation.

The story captivated me through to the end and I really quite enjoyed it.
